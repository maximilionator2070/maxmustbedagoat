Welcome back everyone, this is page two step one. STEP NUMBERO UNO; is to be confident. The saying confidence is key, actually means something because it is an attribute that is important and that can get you to places. To attain confidence you must understand the difference between it and arrogance. Confidence comes from your own self worth, your much more secure with yourself, while arrogance is almost the exact opposite, driven by the insecurity to feel better than others. There is no real instantaneous way to grant yourself confidence, it's something that is built over time. Your experiences should help you obtain it.
